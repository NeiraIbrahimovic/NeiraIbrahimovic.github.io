02 · Connected Multiomics

Turning an acquisition into one product experience.

Product definition, scientific workflow integration, UAT and launch readiness for an Early Access multiomics product.

Context

The integration had to feel bigger than two products stitched together.

I joined before the first Early Access release, shortly after an acquisition. I translated scientific workflows into requirements and user needs, partnered with test engineering on traceability, contributed to UX and extensive UAT, and helped bring workflows spanning areas such as spatial, single-cell and proteomics into a cohesive experience.

Launch readiness

The software was only part of the product.

Usability sessions showed where users naturally looked for actions and exposed gaps that were difficult to see from requirements alone. As launch approached, I also identified a customer-enablement gap: documentation did not have a clear owner. I built the documentation experience and customer-facing demos needed to make Early Access usable, not merely releasable.

What I learnedFor technical products, launch readiness includes requirements, verification, usability, documentation and customer learning. The last mile is part of the product.
“This could not have happened without your contribution.”

Feedback from a test engineering partner who later recognized my work with a spot award.
